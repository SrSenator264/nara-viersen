2. In short

The site is served by our own server at our restaurant and made reachable through a tunnel by Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., USA). Technically necessary connection data (in particular the IP address) is processed to deliver the site and protect it from abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). 4. Fonts (Google Fonts)

For a consistent look, fonts are loaded from Google servers (Google Ireland Ltd., Ireland / Google LLC, USA). Google receives your IP address in the process (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR – consistent presentation).

5. Orders

When you order we process: name, phone number, delivery address (for delivery), order contents, notes, payment method, and order time and status. The purpose is accepting, preparing, delivering and billing your order (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). The data is required for the order; without it we cannot deliver. Delivery drivers see the name, address, phone number and notes of your delivery. Through your order link you can see the status and the driver’s location for your order.

For tax and commercial law reasons we must keep billing data (§ 147 AO, § 257 HGB, up to 10 years depending on the document; Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR). After that we delete it.

6. Payment

Cash and card payments are made on delivery or pickup. For bank transfers we match the incoming payment against the transactions of our business account (amount, reference, sender name).

7. Flamo assistant (AI chat)

If you use the assistant, your message together with your cart context is sent to an AI provider that generates the reply (depending on settings Anthropic PBC or Google, both USA; Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). Please do not enter sensitive data there. Voice output runs in your browser; nothing is sent to us.

8. Storage in your browser

Your browser stores locally (without cookies) your language, cart, the contact details you last entered and the link to your order status. This is necessary for the site to work (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG) and can be deleted at any time in your browser settings.

9. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21 GDPR), and the right to withdraw consent at any time. Please use the contact details above. There is no automated decision-making.
